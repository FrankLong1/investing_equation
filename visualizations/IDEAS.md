# Directions to explore

The first five folders are working starter studies. Keep the numbered framework and source-document links stable while varying the presentation.

- Expanding boxes: animate the actual parent walls opening around their children; retain neighboring stages at the edges.
- Infinite canvas: add semantic zoom from stages to functions to typed input/output objects.
- Orbital worlds: move continuously into a selected world's local constellation.
- Information reactor: trace one clearly labeled illustrative observation through reviewed dependencies, once those connections are explicitly mapped.
- Living blueprint: progressively reveal constraints, deterministic services, and human authority using verified contract metadata.
- Later experiments: exploded technical drawing, cross-section of a machine, architectural rooms, recursive city, and chronological learning loop.

The starter studies show hierarchy, not an asserted dependency graph. Do not derive execution order just from the section numbers or decorative lines. Preserve canonical labels; keep proposed subdivisions visibly provisional.
