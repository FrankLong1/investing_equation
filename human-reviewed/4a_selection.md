# 4a. Selection

Parent: [A Hedge Fund as a Function — Version 4](./hedge-fund-as-a-function-v4.md)

Selection is encompassed by the **2A–2E thesis-formation process**: identify
key debates, understand market expectations, form a variant view, model its
price impact, and build a probability distribution of returns.

That work produces the candidate theses and payoff distributions passed from
[2E](./2e_calculate_expected_returns.md) to the optimizer in
[4B. Position Sizing](./4b_sizing.md). A separate selection workflow is not
needed here. Sizing determines the final weights, including zero, subject to
the investable universe and portfolio constraints.
