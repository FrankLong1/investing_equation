# 4a. Selection

Parent: [A Hedge Fund as a Function — Version 4](./hedge-fund-as-a-function-v4.md)

Research candidate selection is encompassed by the **2A–2E thesis-formation
process**: identify key debates, understand market expectations, form a variant
view, model its price impact, and build a probability distribution of returns.
Those candidate assessments can be reused by funds asking the same dated
research question. They do not authorize a holding in any fund.

That work produces the candidate theses and payoff distributions passed from
[2E](./2e_calculate_expected_returns.md) to the optimizer in
[4B. Position Sizing](./4b_sizing.md). A separate selection workflow is not
needed here. Sizing determines each fund's final weights, including zero,
subject to its investable universe, current portfolio, and constraints.
